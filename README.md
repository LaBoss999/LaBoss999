### 🧑‍💻 About Me

- 🎓 Software Engineering student at **APTECH**
- 🚀 Co-founder & lead developer of **[OJA247](https://github.com/LaBoss999/OJA247)** — a digital marketplace helping Nigerian small businesses bring their existing customers online, with vendor onboarding, split payments, disputes, referrals, and more
- 🧩 I work across the stack and across languages — JavaScript/Node on the web, C#/Java for object-oriented and systems-style work, C for low-level fundamentals
- 🎨 Also bring a **design engineer** lens — comfortable owning a feature from Figma mockup to backend logic to shipped product
- 🧠 Build **image processing** projects in Python (OpenCV / Pillow)
- 💬 Ask me about React, Node/Express, MongoDB, C#/Java, payment integrations, or computer vision
- ⚡ Fun fact: I like solving both pixel-level problems (image processing) and product-level problems (building a marketplace from scratch)

---

### 🚧 Currently Building

Working through OJA247's vendor payments & trust layer: Paystack multi-split payouts, tiered vendor verification (NIN + CAC), a dispute-resolution flow, WhatsApp order alerts via Twilio, and customer accounts with follow/notify. Full changelog lives in the repo.
